# 2.1-SBA-ResponsiveAndAccessibility

What accessibility challenges did you face, and how did you address them?
Having to put yourself in the place of someone who needs to utilize some of the accessibility options available was very challenging. Determining what a screen reader would need to convey to its users was difficult because I would think the more information available the more useful these tools would be however, that is not the case. Users must have concise information to navigate to the sections of a site they need to use without confusion. I was forced to slow down and really consider what information is important and how I could actively help to assist users. I would also say that understanding color theory will serve me well moving forward not only for aestetics but even for accessibilty.

How did you ensure that your design was responsive and accessible to all users?
I tried to keep my design simple so that screen readers would have an easier time parsing through the site. Additionally, i thought it was important to build the site, section by section. The anaolgy of building a house comes to mind when thinking about the approach chosen. I built the HTML first, then CSS and finally accessibilty functions to keep myself organized and to also manage deliverables. Once I completed the site, I went back through the site utilizing Lighthouse to check my score and to see if there were any areas I might've overlooked initially. I work through this loop until everything was properly designed with accessible functionality. Then I applied my @media tags so that my site will be able to maintain its desgn at multiple window sizes. I'll admit that I have more to learn regarding this.

What tools or resources did you find most helpful during this project?
Lighthouse was really helpful with this project for a number of reasons. Initially, I had maybe a third of my aria related labels sprinkled throughout my project. Additionally, some of my text colors didn't work with my chosen backgrounds even though I was able to see the text or border clearly. I never thought about accessibility at all nor did I think about website responsitivity nearly as much before completing this project. This whole experience was eye opening for me.
